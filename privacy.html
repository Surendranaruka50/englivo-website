Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

ENGLIVO is an English learning application intended for adults aged 18 and above. This Privacy Policy explains how information is stored and processed when you use the application.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

ENGLIVO stores certain information locally on your Android device to provide its learning features. This may include:

  • Profile name and selected avatar.
  • Learning progress and completed lessons.
  • Quiz results and scores.
  • Coins, rewards and learning-related progress.
  • App preferences such as sound, music, voice speed and theme.

The audited application source uses Android Room Database and DataStore for local storage.

2. Android Backup

ENGLIVO has Android Backup enabled. Depending on your Android device, Google Account, backup settings and Android system behavior, eligible app data may be included in Android Auto Backup or device-to-device transfer.

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, eligible app data may be transferred to Google's backup service and restored when setting up a compatible device. Backup availability and retention are subject to Android and Google's policies and your device settings.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

ENGLIVO uses Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process information such as:

  • IP address.
  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • Advertisement and app interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and service improvement.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob information .

4. Internet Connection

ENGLIVO uses internet access for advertisements and to check internet connectivity through Google connectivity-check services. The lessons and questions in the audited version are loaded from content bundled inside the application.

5. AI Services and User Accounts

The audited version of ENGLIVO does not contain an active AI Tutor API integration or a direct AI server connection. It does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its learning features.

6. Microphone, Camera and Location

The audited Android manifest does not request microphone, camera or location permissions.

The application uses Android Text-to-Speech functionality for spoken output. This is different from recording the user's voice through a microphone.

7. Third-Party Services

ENGLIVO uses third-party services, particularly Google AdMob and Android/Google connectivity services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Security

ENGLIVO uses Android local application storage for app information. Eligible data may also be included in Android Backup according to your device settings. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app information generally remains on your device until it is deleted by the app, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall the app. Separate backup copies may remain subject to Google's backup retention and deletion practices.

10. Children's Privacy

ENGLIVO is intended for adults aged 18 and above. It is not designed or marketed for children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, data practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on our official website.
